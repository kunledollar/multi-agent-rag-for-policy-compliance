Safety and Health Topics
Wildfires
Wildfires are common disasters that can spread quickly, particularly during dry conditions. learn about wildfire preparedness, response and recovery and the hazards that may be present in areas affected.
Infectious Diseases
Workers may be exposed to infectious diseases that can result in illness and hospitalization.
Common Respiratory Illnesses
Protect workers from exposure to common respiratory viruses including
Influenza, COVID-19,
and
RSV
when they are circulating in the community.
Trenching and Excavation
SLOPE IT. SHORE IT. SHIELD IT.
OSHA is focusing on reducing trenching and excavation hazards. Trench collapses, or cave-ins, pose the greatest risk to workers' lives.
Heat
Many workers become sick or are fatally injured from occupational heat exposure. These illnesses and fatalities are preventable.
Flood Preparedness and Response
Floods can be devasting, learn how to protect your workers responding to flooded areas.
Personal Protective Equipment
Personal protective equipment minimizes exposure to hazards that cause serious workplace injuries and illnesses.
Workplace Violence
A well-written and implemented workplace violence prevention program can reduce the incidence of workplace violence.

Report a Fatality/Severe Injury
All employers are required to notify OSHA when an employee is killed on the job or suffers a work-related hospitalization, amputation, or loss of an eye. A fatality must be reported within 8 hours. An in-patient hospitalization, amputation, or eye loss must be reported within 24 hours.

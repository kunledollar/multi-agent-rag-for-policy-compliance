    Performance Management

    Performance management is a structured approach used to improve the effectiveness, efficiency, and accountability of programs and services. It is the framework used government-wide that helps agencies plan strategically, measure results, and improve how programs and services are delivered to the public. By aligning strategic planning with measurable outcomes, performance management helps agencies meet their missions, manage taxpayer resources responsibly, and continuously improve public service delivery.
